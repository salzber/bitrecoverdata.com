Privacy Policy

How BitsRecover handles, protects, and secures your personal data.

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us via our website, Crisp Chat, Telegram, WhatsApp, or email. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Cryptocurrency wallet addresses and transaction details relevant to your recovery case.
  • Communication history and case notes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and improve our crypto recovery services.
  • Communicate with you regarding your case status and updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure the security of our services.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All client data is treated with strict confidentiality. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your case details are shared only with authorized BitsRecover personnel directly involved in your recovery.

4. Third-Party Communication Platforms

We use third-party platforms such as Crisp Chat, Telegram, and WhatsApp for communication. These platforms have their own privacy policies, and BitsRecover is not responsible for their security practices. We recommend reviewing their policies before using them to share sensitive information.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience and analyze site traffic. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. We do not use cookies to collect personally identifiable information without your consent.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at +1-866-381-7925 or via Telegram.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Case records are typically retained for up to 7 years after case closure for legal and regulatory purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
